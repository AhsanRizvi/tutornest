Features Overview

Designed for Content Creators & Educators

We combine advanced intellectual property defense with immersive, high-quality pedagogy tools to give your students a premium learning experience.

Digital Rights Management (DRM)

Secure video streaming, dynamic screen watermarking with student metadata, and system blockades against screen capture applications keep your videos exactly where they belong.

Real-Time Learning Analytics

Trace watch times, check quiz submission curves, and monitor individual engagement metrics. Identify students who need extra support before exams.

Interactive Lesson Delivery

Combine live webcasts, multi-format quizzes, protected PDF study notes, and assignments inside a beautiful learning portal.

Smart Batches & Scheduling

Organize student cohorts, set automatic timetables for live sessions, and notify classes via text or email with a single click.

Our Philosophy

Educating with Trust, Protected by Technology

At TutorNest, we believe that education is an art. For tutors, their course material represents years of dedicated work, refinement, and unique expertise. In the digital age, this intellectual capital is incredibly fragile, often vulnerable to screen-recording, piracy, and unapproved distribution.

We created TutorNest to restore trust. By surrounding your digital curriculum with highly advanced, unobtrusive DRM safeguards, we allow you to focus entirely on what you do best: inspiring students.
